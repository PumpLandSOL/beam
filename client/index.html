01 · SOURCE

Many exchanges.

Each validator reads prices from its own mix of exchanges: Coinbase, Kraken, OKX, Gemini, Bitfinex, Binance.US, Crypto.com and Bitstamp, plus market data for stocks, FX and metals.

02 · AGREE

One price.

Every round, each validator signs what it saw. The network takes the stake-weighted median, so no single exchange or validator can move a price.

03 · SIGN

Sealed with proof.

Prices are packed into a packet and signed by the validators. More than two thirds of stake has to sign before a packet means anything.

04 · DELIVER

On-chain.

A tunnel carries packets to Robinhood Chain on your schedule, or the moment a price moves. The BeamReceiver contract checks every signature before it stores a thing.

For builders

Two lines to read a price.

Open a tunnel for the feeds you need, point it at a BeamReceiver, and read. Every stored price was signed by more than two thirds of validator stake, and you can check any packet yourself on the Verify page.

Why BEAM

Built for Robinhood Chain.

Stocks, natively.Tesla, NVIDIA, Robinhood and the S&P 500 sit next to BTC and ETH, as first-class feeds, including after-hours prints.
Nobody to trust.A price lands only when validators holding more than two thirds of stake sign it. The contract checks; you don't have to.
Anyone can relay.Packets are self-proving. Our relayer delivers them, and so can you, or anyone else.
Push on your terms.Tunnels update on a heartbeat, on a price move, or both. You choose the feeds, the interval and the threshold.
Open to inspect.Every round, every validator submission and every signature is public in the explorer, as it happens.
Verify in a browser.Paste any packet into Verify and your browser recomputes the hash and recovers every signer itself.
