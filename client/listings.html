List any asset. No one's approval.

On other oracles, the team decides which prices exist. On BEAM, any $BEAM holder can add a feed for a US stock, an ETF or a crypto asset. BEAM checks it against live sources, and the validators start pricing it the very next round. Your listing goes into the stream, the API and any tunnel that asks for it.
