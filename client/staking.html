Stake the oracle. Back who you trust.

Stake $BEAM behind any BEAM validator through the BeamStaking contract on Robinhood Chain. Every staked token is one unit of voting power in the stake-weighted median and in packet quorum. Validators that misreport prices get jailed, and a jailed validator's pool is slashed on-chain and burned.

5% slash when jailed

5 rounds off the median by more than 1.5% within 200 rounds jails a validator. Its whole pool, unbonding included, loses 5%.

7-day unbonding

Unbond any time. Tokens stop counting as power at once and can be withdrawn 7 days later.
