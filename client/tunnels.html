Oracle tunnels

Prices, delivered.

An oracle tunnel is a standing order: these feeds, on this heartbeat, or sooner when a price moves past your threshold. Every update becomes a packet signed by the validator set, ready for the BeamReceiver contract on Robinhood Chain.

Open a tunnel

Sign the request with your wallet. No transaction and no gas: the signature just proves the tunnel is yours, so only you can pause or resume it.
